Research

Five themes, from the interiors of giant planets to Earth's near-space environment.

The group treats magnetic fields and gravity as measurable signals that carry information about places no instrument can reach directly — the metallic interiors of giant planets, the oceans beneath ice shells, and the plasma environments spacecraft fly through. Each theme below pairs the science with the missions that supply the data and the papers that report the results.

Planetary Interiors

How deep do a planet's winds go? Gravity tells us.

Gravity and magnetic measurements reveal deep winds, electrical structure, and interior dynamics. Spacecraft gravity fields, magnetic constraints, and geophysical modeling help infer how atmospheric jets extend below cloud tops, how conducting fluids move inside giant planets, and what those hidden motions reveal about planetary structure and evolution.

Data from  NASA Juno — Hao Cao has been a member of the Juno Science Team since 2014.

Planetary Magnetic Fields

A planet's magnetic field is a readout of the fluid moving inside it.

Dynamo models and spacecraft magnetometry connect evolving fields to fluid motion inside planets. Magnetic-field geometry and time variation from missions such as Juno and Cassini provide constraints on planetary dynamos, secular variation, and the electrically conducting regions where deep flows generate observable magnetic signals.

Data from  NASA Juno · Cassini MAG, where Hao Cao was a team member and Participating Scientist · ESA JUICE J-MAG, as Co-Investigator.

  • Moore, K.M., Cao, H., Bloxham, J., et al. · 2019 — Time-variation of Jupiter's internal magnetic field consistent with zonal wind advection. Nature Astronomy 3, 730–735.Connects secular variation in Jupiter's internal field to zonal wind advection.
  • Cao, H., Dougherty, M.K., Hunt, G.J., et al. · 2020 — The landscape of Saturn's internal magnetic field from the Cassini Grand Finale. Icarus 344, 113541.Uses Cassini Grand Finale magnetometry to resolve fine-scale structure in Saturn's internal field.
  • Dougherty, M.K., Cao, H., Khurana, K.K., et al. · 2018 — Saturn's magnetic field revealed by the Cassini Grand Finale. Science 362, eaat5434.Reports the close-in Saturn magnetic-field measurements central to the dynamo work.

Ocean Worlds

A salty ocean under ice responds to a changing magnetic field — and that response is measurable from orbit.

Magnetic induction and plasma interactions probe icy moons and coupled interior–space environments. Electromagnetic responses help characterize subsurface oceans, ice shells, and the plasma environments around moons such as Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto, linking interior structure with the surrounding magnetospheric system.

Data from  ESA JUICE J-MAG, as Co-Investigator · NASA Europa Clipper, via the JUICE–Clipper Steering Committee.

Surface Magnetometry

Building the instrument, not just analysing the data.

Instrument development supports long-duration magnetic measurements on the lunar surface. The miniaturized, low-power fluxgate magnetometer systems built for surface observatories provide sustained magnetic records for investigating electrical structure, thermal state, and interactions with plasma environments.

Supported by  NASA DALI project L-MAG, with Hao Cao as Principal Investigator since April 2024.

Key publications

  • Cao, H., Kuang, W., Cheng, J.S. and Aurnou, J.M. · 2024 — Revealing whole Earth dynamics through geomagnetism: From core to magnetosphere. White paper for the NASA Earth Science CORE 2.0 Report.Connects magnetic observations from surface platforms to broad geophysical inference.
  • Cao, H., Yadav, R.K. and Aurnou, J.M. · 2018 — Geomagnetic polar minima do not arise from steady meridional circulation. PNAS 115, 11186–11191.Methods for separating interior dynamics from measured surface signals.

Space Weather

Where and how the solar wind gets into Earth's magnetic field.

Fluxgate magnetometers help measure Earth's near-space response to solar-wind forcing. The TRACERS mission and related instrument work apply magnetic-field measurements to study reconnection, polar cusp dynamics, and the way solar-wind energy enters Earth's magnetosphere.

Supported by  NASA TRACERS, with Hao Cao as Deputy Instrument Lead and Co-Investigator on the MAG team since February 2024.
